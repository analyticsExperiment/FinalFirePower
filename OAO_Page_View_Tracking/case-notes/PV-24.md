# PV-24 · Auto-declined outcome screen

The overview includes an auto-declined outcome. The ticket’s decisioning conditions govern which screen is displayed. The displayed decline screen receives its approved page view.

Use **Required payload checks** in the workbook for the seven fields. Match each screen’s approved inventory identity and compare its data-layer values with Network and Adobe Assurance.

**Comments.** Use a decline fixture and capture the rendered screen. **Actual Result** and **Screenshot** are blank for UAT. **Status** is **Not run**.
