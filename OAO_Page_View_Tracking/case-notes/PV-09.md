Configure Account is shared by the new-customer application and existing-customer new-account branches. Both displayed instances are covered. The approved inventory decides whether route-specific conditions change the mapping.

Use **Required payload checks** in the workbook for the seven fields. Match each screen’s approved inventory identity and compare its data-layer values with Network and Adobe Assurance.
