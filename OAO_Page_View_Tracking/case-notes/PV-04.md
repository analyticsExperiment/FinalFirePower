# PV-04 · Manual Product Selection from CIT.com

Start from CIT.com/OAO Welcome. Open Product Selection as a new or existing customer and choose the product and rate code.

**Expected.** Expect one pageView when Product Selection is visible. Changing selections on the same screen adds no pageView.

**Why.** The CIT.com flows show product and code selection on Product Selection. Red values need client confirmation. Actual values are for UAT.
