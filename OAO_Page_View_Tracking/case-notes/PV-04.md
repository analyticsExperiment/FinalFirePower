# PV-04 · Manual Product Selection from CIT.com

The regular Welcome flows require manual product and code selection. The ticket tracks the logical screen rendering and puts product interaction events in their own scope. The page view keeps the screen’s approved identity while selections change.

Use **Required payload checks** in the workbook for the seven fields. Match each screen’s approved inventory identity and compare its data-layer values with Network and Adobe Assurance.

**Comments.** Follow the sign-in/OTP path when required for the existing customer. Compare both runs to the approved mapping. **Actual Result** and **Screenshot** are blank for UAT. **Status** is **Not run**.

**Sources.** Page-view ticket, Scope, §3.6 and AC7. Rate Promotion flows 2, 4, 6 and 8.
