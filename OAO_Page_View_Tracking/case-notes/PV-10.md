# PV-10 · Joint Applicant Information Page 1

Flow 2 includes joint applicant pages after Configure Account. Choosing joint ownership exposes this conditional step. Its identity must be the inventory entry for Joint Applicant Information Page 1.

Use **Required payload checks** in the workbook for the seven fields. Match each screen’s approved inventory identity and compare its data-layer values with Network and Adobe Assurance.

**Comments.** Use a joint UAT application and approved applicant data. **Actual Result** and **Screenshot** are blank for UAT. **Status** is **Not run**.

**Sources.** Page-view ticket, AC1 and AC4. Rate Promotion flow 2.
