# PV-18 · Funding with Mail-in Check

Mail-in Check is the funding method shown in the detailed landing and existing-customer flows. It makes this setup specific. The page view describes the visible Funding screen and uses its inventory identity.

Use **Required payload checks** in the workbook for the seven fields. Match each screen’s approved inventory identity and compare its data-layer values with Network and Adobe Assurance.

**Comments.** Use an allowed UAT amount. Capture the screen’s approved mapping. **Actual Result** and **Screenshot** are blank for UAT. **Status** is **Not run**.
