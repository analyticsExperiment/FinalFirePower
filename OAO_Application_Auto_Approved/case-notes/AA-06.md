# AA-06 · New customer opens a custodial account

The custodial example covers both customer types. The field rules resolve the new-customer variant to `New`, `ambiguous` and a literal `null` IDP. `Custodial` and `custodial` come from the classification and ownership rules. The fixture supplies the approved product and funding data.

Use **Required payload checks** for the shared fields. Compare the push with Network and Adobe Assurance and reconcile the Application ID in OSP.

**Comments.** Use a new-customer custodial fixture. Capture the assigned Application ID. **Actual Result** and **Screenshot** are blank for UAT. **Status** is **Not run**.
