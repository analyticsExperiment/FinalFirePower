# AA-08 · Auto-approved application funded by Wire Transfer

Wire Transfer appears in the ticket’s funding method examples. The Funding selection supplies `fundingMethod`. The finish result supplies `fundingStatus` and `initialDepositAmount`, and the ticket specifies `USD`.

Use **Required payload checks** for the shared fields. Compare the push with Network and Adobe Assurance and reconcile the Application ID in OSP.

**Comments.** Needs a Wire Transfer UAT fixture. Record the selected method and the finish-page status and amount. **Actual Result** and **Screenshot** are blank for UAT. **Status** is **Not run**.

**Sources.** Forwarded ticket, §3.2 (funding attributes) and Acceptance Criteria, Scenario 1.
